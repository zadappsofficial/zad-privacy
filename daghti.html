English

Zad Daghti ("Daghti", "the App") is developed by ZAD. Your privacy matters to us.

Data we collect

  • No account. The App works fully offline and does not require registration, login, or any personal information from you.
  • On-device data only. Your blood pressure and pulse readings, notes, reminder settings, family profiles and statistics are stored only on your device (AsyncStorage). They never leave your phone and are deleted if you uninstall the App. We do not collect, transmit, or store your health readings on any server.
  • Purchases. If you buy Zad Pro, the purchase is processed by Google Play / RevenueCat. We receive only an anonymized entitlement flag ("zad_pro active") — no payment details.
  • Advertising. The free version shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers to serve ads; requests are configured as non-personalized with a PG content rating. Buying Zad Pro disables all ads permanently. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob data practices.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect names, emails, precise location, contacts, photos, or any health data from your device sensors. The App requests notification permission only to deliver your measurement reminders.

Medical disclaimer

Daghti is a personal tracking tool for awareness and organizing your readings only. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice. Do not rely on it in emergencies — always consult your doctor about your health.

Children

The App is rated Everyone and contains no user-generated content or social features.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the App and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
