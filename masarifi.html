English

Zad Masarifi ("Masarifi", "the App") is developed by ZAD. Your privacy matters to us.

Data we collect

  • No account. The App works fully offline and does not require registration, login, or any personal information from you.
  • On-device data. Your expenses, custom categories, monthly budgets, and spending statistics are stored only on your device (AsyncStorage). They never leave your phone and are deleted if you uninstall the App. Any backup file you export is created by you and stays under your control.
  • Purchases. If you buy Zad Pro, the purchase is processed by Google Play / RevenueCat. We receive only an anonymized entitlement flag ("zad_pro active") — no payment details.
  • Advertising. The free version shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers to serve ads; requests are configured as non-personalized with a PG content rating. Buying Zad Pro disables all ads permanently. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob data practices.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect names, emails, precise location, contacts, photos, or financial account credentials. Your expense records are personal and remain on your device. The App requests notification permission only to deliver your daily expense reminder and budget alerts.

Children

The App is rated Everyone and contains no user-generated content or social features.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the App and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

Contact

Questions: zadappsofficial@gmail.com

  • بلا حساب. يعمل التطبيق دون إنترنت ولا يطلب تسجيلاً أو أي معلومات شخصية.
  • بيانات على جهازك فقط. مصاريفك وفئاتك المخصصة وميزانياتك الشهرية وإحصائيات إنفاقك تُحفظ على جهازك فقط وتُحذف بإلغاء تثبيت التطبيق. أي ملف نسخ احتياطي تصدّره أنت يبقى تحت سيطرتك.
