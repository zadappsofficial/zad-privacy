Privacy Policy — زكاتي | Zad Zakah

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Developer: ZAD

Contact: zadappsofficial@gmail.com

Overview

Zad Zakah («زكاتي») is an offline Zakah calculator. We designed it so that

no personal data ever leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or store on any server:

  • names, emails, phone numbers, or any identifiers;
  • the financial amounts, gold weights, or any values you enter;
  • your Zakah payment history — it is saved only in your device's local storage.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (calculator inputs, default prices, payment log, settings)

is stored locally on your device using the operating system's app storage.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. We cannot access it.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob may show ads (non-personalized requests only). AdMob is

operated by Google and subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Ads are disabled

entirely for Pro users and no ad identifiers are linked to your entries.

  • RevenueCat processes the optional one-time Pro purchase. Purchase

processing is handled by Google Play; we receive only the entitlement status

(Pro / not Pro).

Children's privacy

The app contains no age-restricted content and collects no data from anyone,

including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL

before the change takes effect.
