Privacy Policy — مائي | Zad Maii

Developer: ZAD

Last updated: 2026-10-19

English

Maii ("the App") is developed by ZAD. Your privacy matters.

Data we collect: None. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All your data — water logs, weight, cup sizes, reminder settings — is stored locally on your device only (AsyncStorage) and never leaves it.

Accounts: The App has no accounts and no sign-in.

Advertising: The App may display ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers per Google's policies. Ads are non-personalized. You can remove ads permanently with an optional one-time purchase (Zad Pro) via RevenueCat.

Children: The App is rated Everyone and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes: If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the App update.
